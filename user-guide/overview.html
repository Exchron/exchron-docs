User Guide Overview

Welcome! This guide gives you a quick orientation to the two user modes in Exchron—Playground and Classroom—plus where to go next.

What is the Playground?​

The Playground is an interactive space to experiment with models and inputs, see instant results, and iterate quickly.

  • Who it’s for: Explorers and tinkerers who want to try different inputs and see how predictions change.
  • What you can do:
    • Adjust parameters manually (sliders/inputs) or upload a CSV
    • Compare outputs and view supporting charts/metrics
    • Follow a guided flow that shows what to try next
  • How it works: Your inputs are processed in the browser, then the app renders a simulated result with clear explanations. Nothing is saved unless you export it.

See the step-by-step guide: user-guide/playground-flow

What is the Classroom?​

The Classroom is a structured, stepwise learning flow that walks through a simple ML lifecycle.

  • Who it’s for: Learners and presenters who want a clear, scaffolded sequence with checkpoints.
  • What you can do:
    • Load or upload a dataset
    • Choose a model and set basic hyperparameters
    • Step through training and see progress
    • Test and export the result
  • How it works: Each step provides focused instructions and visuals, with simulated metrics so you can understand the flow end-to-end.

See the walkthrough: user-guide/classroom-flow

Tips​

  • Use the sidebar to jump between steps if you’re revisiting something.
