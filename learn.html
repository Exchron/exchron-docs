Exchron Learn Platform

Exchron Learn is the educational companion to the Exchron Dashboard. It helps students and curious explorers discover the worlds beyond our solar system through approachable articles, interactive visuals, and an exoplanet simulator.

  • Tagline (from the site): “Discover the mysterious worlds beyond our solar system. A platform dedicated to exploring and learning about exoplanets and the wonders they hold.”
  • Aim: “Enlighten students through innovative, engaging, and accessible learning experiences that transform complex scientific concepts into captivating visualizations and interactive tools.”

External site: https://learn.exchronai.earth/

Who is it for?​

  • Students and educators using Exchron to learn and teach exoplanet concepts
  • Anyone who wants a read‑first, guided introduction before using the Dashboard

Key areas you’ll find​

  • Start Your Journey: A guided “Overview of Exoplanets” (CTA points to /overview/exoplanets on the Learn site)
  • Exoplanet Simulator: Explore orbital properties and planetary scales interactively
  • ML on Exoplanets (intro): Learn how machine learning helps classify new exoplanets; pointers to try classifying real data or building your own model in the Dashboard
  • Get in touch: info.exchron@gmail.com

Links observed on the site:

  • Home: https://learn.exchronai.earth/home
  • Overview: (menu section)
  • Exoplanet Simulator: https://learn.exchronai.earth/exoplanet-simulator
  • Exchron Dashboard: http://dashboard.exchronai.earth
  • Documentation: (link on Learn landing) http://docs.exchronai.earth

How it relates to the Dashboard​

  • Learn vs Playground: Learn focuses on understanding concepts (read, watch, simulate). The Dashboard’s Playground lets you experiment with model inputs and see predictions immediately.
  • Learn vs Classroom: Learn provides conceptual grounding and visuals. The Dashboard’s Classroom walks you step‑by‑step through a simplified ML workflow (data → model → train → test → export).

See also:

  • Playground Flow: /user-guide/playground-flow
  • Classroom Flow: /user-guide/classroom-flow

Getting started​

  1. Visit Exchron Learn: https://learn.exchronai.earth/
  2. Click “Start Your Journey” to open the Overview of Exoplanets
  3. Try the Exoplanet Simulator to explore orbits and scales
  4. Read the ML intro section to connect concepts with model classification
  5. When ready to apply what you’ve learned, open the Exchron Dashboard and follow the Playground or Classroom guides

Tip: Keep Learn open alongside the Dashboard so you can reference explanations while you experiment or train models.
