Exoplanet Simulator

The Exoplanet Simulator on Exchron Learn helps you build intuition for how planets move and how their properties compare.

What you can explore:

  • Orbits: Change orbital period, distance, and inclination to see how paths vary
  • Scales: Compare planet sizes and stellar sizes visually
  • Visual cues: Use overlays to connect parameters with the shapes you see

When to use it:

  • Before training/testing models, to ground your understanding of transit signals
  • In classrooms, to demonstrate concepts live with sliders and toggles

Where to find it: https://learn.exchronai.earth/exoplanet-simulator
